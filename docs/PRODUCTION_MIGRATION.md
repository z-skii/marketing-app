# V3 production migration

`c894df1`) migrated into the real product on the branch
`claude/v3-production-migration`. Nothing here is merged: the branch waits
for the founder's approval. This document is the record the approval is
made against.

## 2. What was not copied blindly

The lab depends on the V2 Lab's stylesheet, primitives and fixtures, on
its own lab controls, and on two fonts loaded at the root plus two more
in the Frame Shift group. The production build keeps the approved
appearance and drops the dependence:

- **Fonts.** DM Sans is the only font, loaded once in the root layout.
  Inter and IBM Plex Mono (root), Archivo and IBM Plex Sans (Frame Shift)
  are gone. The monospace stack falls back to the system stack.
- **CSS.** `src/v3/v3.css` is generated by `scripts/v3-css.mjs` from the
  seven lab stylesheets, keeping only the rules whose classes the
  production V3 sources use (972 rules kept, 1151 dropped: the V2 Lab's
  own screens, the Loyalty simulation, the lab controls). Frame Shift
  (`frame-shift.css`) is retokened to the V3 material (canvas, ink, focus
  green, 12/18px radii, the floating lens tab bar, DM Sans) and loads only
  on the signed in screens, not at the root.
- **Components.** `src/v3` holds the engine (Film, LateImg, Near, Open,
  Stage, Plan, motion), the site scenes, the V3 primitives (Sheet, Viewer,
  Img, Wordmark, Money) and the example data. Lab only parts (LabControl,
  the lab entrance, Outside, the V2 shells, the Loyalty store) were not
  ported. The Drive and Loyalty scenes remain islands (loaded within
  1200px of the viewport); the reference is the only preloaded image;
  below the fold media loads late; reduced motion renders every sequence
  in document flow; Pause motion stays on every surface with motion.

## 3. Public homepage (`src/app/page.tsx`)

The approved V3 film on real routes. Sign in and Sign up are `/sign-in`
and `/sign-up`; a signed in visitor gets Open TapMart; the footer links
are `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/creator-terms`, `/rules`; the plans chapter
reads `PLANS` and the live prices; Get paid reads the live platform fee
and payout minimum and derives the recorded example's gross and fee from
them. The scenes render example media (people, businesses, campaigns and
amounts) and say so: the strip beneath the navigation, the footer note,
and the About the examples sheet. No lab label, lab route, fixture name
or "Outside this preview" boundary reaches a visitor (audited by grep
and by reading every rendered page's text). Loyalty is presented as a
coming soon feature: no preview link, no Wallet issuance claim, the
Wallet cards labelled as examples.

## 4. Signed in surfaces

- **User Home** (`/home`): the real open campaigns as objects on the dark
  stage, one lead per kind, the rest in the same language; real filters,
  ordering, paging, resume line and city prompt; the plane opens
  `/o/[id]`; Save is the real toggle; money is `pay_cents` with its real
  basis; Apply by and spots come from the record.
- **User Profile** (`/me`): real identity (avatar or initial, name,
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL/u/username`, city, completed, rating), Instagram
  state, verification, the person's own submissions as planes tagged with
  kind and status, the real vehicle with its honest state (3D model only
  when built, Photos only, Scan in progress, No photos yet), rows for
  Instagram, verification, Earnings, Activity, Portfolio, public profile,
  shoots and Settings; the gear opens Settings.
- **Business Home** (`/business`): real people (portrait, up to two of
  their own samples, provenance, recorded facts, View person, Request
  which opens the real request flow) and real listed cars (photograph,
  placements, asking rate, View); real attention lines and filters; a
  Loyalty row marked Coming soon.
- **Loyalty** (`/business/loyalty`): a coming soon shell behind the real
  business context. No program, member, count or Wallet pass is created,
  claimed or shown as working.
- **Other routes**: every Frame Shift screen (Activity, Earnings,
  Messages, Notifications, Search, Content, Create, Campaigns, Business,
  Settings and their subpages) renders in the retokened material (DM
  Sans, V3 canvas and ink, the floating lens tab bar, ink primary
  actions, green focus). The older (v2) route group (the personal
  settings, edit profile, Instagram, portfolio, creator verification,
  vehicles and the vehicle scan, the business add, edit, connections,
  social, trends, shoots pages; the rest of the group redirects into the
  Frame Shift screens) shares the same presentation layer through the
  global tokens: those tokens (`--tm-*` in globals.css) now carry the V3
  canvas, paper, ink, focus green, radii, shadows and the light lens;
  the V2 chrome shows the product wordmark, an ink Create control and a
  neutral active state; buttons, fields, pills, tags and the bottom bar
  follow. The sign in, sign up, reset and onboarding screens (AuthShell)
  and the legal pages (LegalPage, now with the product wordmark and Sign
  in) render in the same material. Workflows are unchanged. Internal
  routes (admin, the labs, the legacy board) are untouched.

## 7. Preview deployment

Every push to the branch builds a Vercel preview. The current one is
recorded in the report. Preview deployments of this project have no
`DATABASE_URL` in the Preview environment (the previous lab previews
returned 500 at `/` for the same reason), so database backed routes,
including the homepage, error there until the Preview environment gets
the same database variables as Production. Adding them is the founder's
call; nothing in this branch changes environment configuration.

## 8. Not done, by design

- No merge to `main`, no production deployment, no database change.
- `src/legacy`, `/design-lab`, `/design-lab-v2`, `/design-lab-v3` stay.
  `scripts/v3-css.mjs` reads the lab stylesheets, so the lab CSS files
  must remain until the generated `src/v3/v3.css` is made the source.
- No director review was run: the production captures match the approved
  lab stills (same composition, material and type), so there was no drift
  to review.
