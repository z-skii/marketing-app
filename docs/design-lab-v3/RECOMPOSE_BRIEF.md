# V3 recomposition brief

Recorded 2026-09-18 from the founder's review of the V3 package
(docs/design-lab-v3/V3_PACKAGE.html). Nothing here migrates to production.
The V3 lab at /design-lab-v3 stays the only place this work exists until the
founder approves it.

## What is approved and stays

The V3 product architecture and the UX simplification are approved to keep:

- the reduced text and the show, do not explain discipline
- the Recreate, Story and Drive logic and the truth rules around them
- the User and Business structure and the two worlds on one page
- the simplified settings architecture
- the Loyalty architecture (first touch attribution, member model, visits,
  unlock, redemption, QR, Wallet concepts, privacy, aggregates)
- the campaign to customer to repeat customer loop
- truthful states, accessibility, the pause control and reduced motion
- the existing backend and product logic, the fixtures and the media bindings
  in docs/design-lab-v3/MEDIA_MANIFEST.json

The product is not restarted. Flows and data do not change.

## What is not approved

The visual experience. The founder's read of the current V3: still a clean
editorial web prototype. A flat white and cream canvas, dark green, straight
photography, basic text layouts, normal web sections, conventional UI.
Correct, but not memorable, and not a launch.

The founder wants a premium, spatial, animated product experience that a
visitor has not seen before. This is a visual, motion and presentation
recomposition of the same product.

## The direction

### One connected product film

The public homepage is one connected interactive product film, not stacked
sections. Objects continue between chapters: the hero Reel becomes the
Recreate reference; it expands into the creator's submission; approval
resolves into earnings; the world moves into Story; the Story enters the
customer's phone; the customer enters the Loyalty sequence. Connected, not
stacked.

### Hero

A major recompose. A visitor understands in seconds, without paragraphs,
"I can make money" and "I can grow my business". One striking spatial
composition of real product objects: creator, Reel, Story, vehicle, earnings,
business, loyalty card. Depth and motion. Worthy of a modern product launch.

### Glass and material

Pushed much further, with Apple level restraint and depth: a translucent
navigation, layered planes, subtle refraction, content moving beneath glass,
edge highlights, selective floating sheets, soft physical shadows and
transitions between opaque and translucent. Not glass everywhere. The
interface should feel physical.

### Recreate, wow moment one

REFERENCE, YOUR VERSION, APPROVED, EARNED as one transformation of the same
media object. The amount is the natural conclusion. Not four screenshots.

### Story

The 9:16 creative moves into a phone and social context. Posted, proof,
approval, earnings through movement, not labels.

### Drive, wow moment two

The current presentation (a generic silver car and a line drawing) is not
acceptable. A beautifully art directed real vehicle. Zones directly on or
around the vehicle. The vehicle enters, the camera shifts, the zones reveal,
the business creative attaches to a zone, campaign and pay material appears,
the monthly earning resolves. Without production 3D: coded layers,
perspective, masking and photography. Production 3D is never faked. A future
Smart Vehicle may appear as a clearly labelled concept. No crude generic car
diagram as the centrepiece.

### Business Home

People and cars as valuable inventory: portrait, work, personality, quick
request; vehicle, location, zone, rate, request. Not a directory, a CRM or a
classified listing. (Not part of the first build.)

### Profile

Keep the architecture, make it more aspirational: media as identity,
shareable as tapmart.live/@username. (Not part of the first build.)

### Loyalty, wow moment three

Keep the architecture. One visual chain: CREATOR or CAMPAIGN, CUSTOMER,
WALLET CARD, VISIT, RETURN, REWARD, ATTRIBUTION. The moment the returning
customer's original creator or campaign becomes visible should feel
powerful. No paragraphs.

### Image quality

Better art directed media for vehicles, creator work, businesses, Story
creatives and campaign media. High quality generated supporting photography
where the lab needs it, clearly fictional. Astra rejects mediocre assets.

### Typography

Stronger scale, rhythm and contrast without losing readability. Not
everything huge.

### Motion, the biggest missing layer

Shared element transitions, mask reveals, object persistence, scroll linked
choreography, depth transitions, parallax only where it means something,
physical sheets, spring based interactions, cross chapter object handoff. No
gimmicks: the product is what moves.

## How Astra judges it

Not "is it premium". The six questions:

1. Would someone remember this website tomorrow?
2. Could this homepage belong to any other startup?
3. Do Recreate, Story and Drive each have a recognizable physical identity?
4. Does the car moment make TapMart feel unique?
5. Does the Loyalty loop make a business understand why TapMart is more than
   creator marketing?
6. Would someone scroll because they want to see what happens next?

Astra receives the current V3 captures and recordings and the real Artec
study, is told explicitly that the architecture is approved and the current
visual presentation is not, and is asked to increase spatial quality,
material quality, motion continuity, distinctiveness and emotional impact
without adding text or changing the product.

## The first build, and only the first build

Before any other V3 screen is touched, rebuild only:

1. the public hero
2. the Recreate sequence
3. the Drive sequence
4. the Business to Loyalty sequence

User Home, Profile, Business Home and the rest of the app wait.

For each of the four: a desktop still, a mobile still and an actual screen
recording, not only frame strips.

## Stop point

Show: the new hero still and recording; the new Recreate still and
recording; the new Drive still and recording; the new Business to Loyalty
still and recording; Astra's comparison against the current V3; the
performance impact. Then wait for approval. Do not migrate.
