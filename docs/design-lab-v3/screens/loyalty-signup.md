# V3 art direction: Customer signup and Wallet card

By Astra, TapMart's design director.

**Two seconds.** Loopday offers a free coffee after five qualifying visits. Join with a first name and one contact; the next object is your own demo member card, not an account dashboard.

**Signature.** An ordinary loyalty product would not keep a creator’s acquisition thread quietly attached while a signup becomes a distinct member QR and then a returning customer’s card. Here, From Jasmine’s Story. survives outside the pass through every example state. It is continuity, not a referral badge, discount promise or creator-facing customer record.
