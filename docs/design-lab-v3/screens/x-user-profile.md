## two second read

Maya Chen, her own work and her real car. An identity worth sharing, with nothing private on display.

## thesis

Make the profile an authored presence rather than an account page: one generous portrait establishes Maya, a short identity line establishes who she is, and a loose arrangement of her actual work supplies the reason to choose her. A smaller vehicle contact sheet completes the identity without competing with the work. Bright canvas, precise typography and uncovered photography do most of the visual work; material depth belongs only to navigation and active inspection. Sharing reveals this same composition without owner controls, while one Settings gear contains administration.

## engineering notes

- Use the supplied V3 material and typography system, not the current capture’s warm paper, heavy display type, shared media baseline or brick terminal. Bundle the existing DM Sans locally; do not introduce a new display face.
- Canvas is z0; media z10; opaque reading and task surfaces z20; navigation and inspection controls z40; primary grip controls z50. Modal scrim is z60 and the modal shell z70. A task header’s local lens sits above its own body without nesting backdrop filters.
- All page coordinates below describe the default 100% text-size baseline. Use normal document flow and intrinsic dimensions, not absolute-positioned content. Recalculate downstream heights when real image ratios, public facts or wrapping change.
- Resolve the actual work files, public context, ownership, image dimensions and lettering before implementation approval. Record exact filenames and reviewed crops in MEDIA_MANIFEST.json. Do not treat the current screenshots as proof of an image-to-record relationship.
- Reserve every image’s dimensions before loading. Portrait uses a reviewed 4:5 presentation; authored creative retains its full original frame. The 4:5, 9:16 and 3:2 gallery dimensions below are planning envelopes contingent on the inspected source ratios, never permission to cut artwork or stretch it.
- Keep money and administration out of profile data presented to the public composition. Guest mode must not simply hide owner controls visually while leaving them focusable. Use the existing public visibility model for identity, work and vehicle.
- Use object-bound route state for selected work, vehicle and overlays; preserve the public-view parameter when opening media. Record actual implemented query keys in FILE_MAP.md rather than claiming guessed routes exist.
- For shared-object transitions, measure source and destination once, reserve the final layout, and animate a registered image plane through transforms and clipping. Never interpolate image geometry by nonuniform scaling. Text appears at its final readable scale rather than stretching with media.
- Do not animate width, height, top or left every frame. No repeated layout reads during motion, no idle requestAnimationFrame loop and no permanent will-change on every image. A still-only profile needs zero active video decoders.
- There is no simulated optical refraction on this surface. Genuine backdrop blur on navigation and an active inspection rail is sufficient; the source-image rim approximation remains restricted to the three controlled public stages.
- Allow at most two active backdrop-filter regions on phone and three on desktop; this profile should normally use one and at most two. Target visible blur area below 20% of the viewport. Do not blur or refract photographs, work lettering or form bodies.
- Use opaque #FFFFFF when reduced transparency, unavailable filtering or a difficult background makes a light lens unreadable. Require at least 4.5:1 contrast for its visible labels throughout scrolling. Do not darken the photography to rescue control contrast.
- At 320px use 12px page padding, a portrait no wider than 236px and a single-column work gallery when two columns would make artwork unreadable. At 200% text zoom use natural-height header groups, stacked facts and a one-column gallery; do not enforce the baseline y coordinates.
- At 768 to 1023px retain the 80px labelled owner rail, 24px main padding and an eight-column grid: identity three columns, work five. Put Personal in the main header. At 1024px and above retain the 200px sidebar. At 1920px cap owner main content at 1280px.
- All interactive targets are at least 44×44px. Use 2px #315E49 focus outlines with 3px offset on light surfaces. Gallery items are real operable buttons or links with ownership-specific accessible names, not clickable decorative images.
- Give inline work inspection a labelled region and a focusable heading, not a modal focus trap. Share, Settings and vehicle tasks use modal semantics, background inertness, contained focus and a visible Close. Escape dismisses only the topmost relevant layer.
- Use responsive derivatives of the approved source media only. Start with an approximately 80 to 100KB portrait and 70 to 100KB leading work image where visual quality permits; defer below-fold vehicle and lower gallery assets. Target initial route media at or below 350KB, but report actual transfer size and any justified quality exception.
- Performance gates: target LCP at or below 2.5 seconds, CLS at or below 0.1 and measured interaction-to-next-paint at or below 200ms on the named test setup. Controls must acknowledge input immediately even while a 440ms presentation transition continues.
- Count actual visible text after fonts and images settle, including repeated labels and image lettering. In state inventories, replacement-region strings supplement any unobscured base strings; they are not a shortcut to excluding the background from a visible count.
- No repository inspection, browser capture, ownership verification or performance test is claimed by this direction. Unresolved source mappings or unsupported states block the relevant approval rather than authorizing a plausible substitute.
