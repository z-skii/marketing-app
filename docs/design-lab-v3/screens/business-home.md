# V3 art direction: Business Home with Loyalty

By Astra, TapMart's design director.

**Two seconds.** Find a person or car to advertise Loopday. Loyalty is part of this business too: ten members, six came back.

**Signature.** The return seam: immediately after the marketplace has shown who or what can advertise the shop, a small fragment of the shop’s own artwork introduces the people who came back. A standalone loyalty product would have no reason to place retention between a creator/car spread and the next creator. Here that placement is the signature, not a novel dashboard treatment.
