## Risks

- Evidence gate: these are stills and sampled recording strips, not actual recordings available for temporal review. They support composition and state comparisons, not claims of smoothness, dwell quality or uninterrupted handoffs. Require the eight desktop/phone recordings after fixes, including forward and reverse operation, settled endpoints, Pause motion, direct steps and keyboard use. Current captures are also much smaller than the recomposition captures, so their relative sharpness is not a fair quality comparison.
- Mobile performance remains an approval blocker. Reported LCP improves slightly from 4.7 seconds to 4.3 to 4.6 seconds, and Lighthouse moves from 83 to 83 to 85, but LCP still misses the 2.5-second target. First-load media rises from 237KB to 425KB: 188KB, approximately 79%, more, and 75KB above the 350KB direction budget. Preserve the reference's first-paint priority, right-size supporting derivatives and defer the full Drive asset.
- The performance cost is modest on desktop but not zero: reported Lighthouse 100 to 99 and LCP 0.7 to 0.8 to 0.9 seconds. Reported CLS remains 0. Mobile TBT rises from 40ms to 50 to 80ms; TBT is not measured INP. Desktop scroll p50 of 16.7ms and p95 of 16.7 to 33.3ms with no frame above 50ms are encouraging, but do not establish uniform 60fps or phone motion performance. Supply matched hardware/network conditions, interaction measurements and paint/memory evidence.
- Visual truth must survive hierarchy, not merely exist in small print. Counter pour's approval is not a ledger credit; the Drive specimen is not installed advertising; Jasmine's 4/3/1 figures are recorded aggregates, not counters generated by Sara's replay. Keep the adjacent bases and qualifications readable at the strongest visual moments.
- Final accessibility and state-isolation evidence is still required: reduced-motion ordered flow, pause behavior, reduced transparency, focus restoration, browser Back, 320px layouts, 200% text zoom and rendered text counts. Confirm public replay leaves the working Loyalty baseline untouched. A visible Pause motion label alone does not prove the behavior.
- Direction approval is not authorization to expand scope. Finish and review these four public experiences in /design-lab-v3, then stop for founder approval. Do not redesign Story's interior, User Home, Profile or Business Home, and do not migrate to production.
