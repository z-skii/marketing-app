## Still a web prototype

It still reads as an editorial web prototype at the light masthead, the isolated bottom-left action row and the reset into a titled Recreate stage with a changed media silhouette. It does not read that way in the strongest hero area: the reference, attached amount, portrait and partially occluded Story now form an actual spatial hierarchy rather than a photograph beside copy. This is a meaningful recomposition, not merely a recolor; targeted corrections are appropriate.

## Spatial read

Mobile preserves the face and makes the reference commanding, but its terms sheet is over-occluded and the business actions lack a clear spatial owner. Fix those relationships without adding objects, captions, filters or larger type. Scores use a 1 to 10 scale; higher is better except slop_risk, and downstream-experience scores remain provisional because this package reviews the hero only.

The sheet's contact edge is stronger than pass 1, but its reading plane must remain exposed.

- 2. **Desktop and phone, top of page through the hero-to-Recreate boundary..** Extend the dark hero substrate behind the navigation and lab/motion band. Keep one navigation lens and exchange its backing only when the inspection substrate actually reaches it. Why: The captures still show a light 120px masthead above the dark composition. A dark navigation fill sitting on a light header is not the specified lens over the hero; it preserves the conventional website-header-plus-feature-section split.
- 3. **Hero-to-Recreate handoff and its reverse, both viewports..** Retain the reviewed 4:5 crop as the reference wrapper enters Recreate. Scale and translate that carrier to the 416×520px desktop or 326×407.5px phone endpoint without exposing a taller crop. Preserve the intact 9:16 source for its separate inspection entrance. Why: The strips show the hero's 4:5 reference resolving into a visibly taller portrait aperture. That changes the object's silhouette at the exact point where persistence should be most convincing.
- 4. **Desktop reverse handoff, especially the light-to-dark substrate exchange..** Keep the departing hero typography on its dark reading substrate until it exits, or occlude it before exposing the light stage. Make the reverse transition obey the same rule. Do not leave white audience controls visible against the light inspection substrate. Why: The penultimate desktop strip frame shows white Make money and Grow your business on the light stage. This is an actual intermediate-frame contrast failure, not merely a timing concern.
- 5. **Business hero, primarily desktop; retain the same ownership relationship on phone..** Bring the business-lens person/actions sheet into visible contact with Maya's portrait-and-work grouping, while leaving the campaign reference separately labelled and dominant. Reposition the existing sheet rather than adding a connector or explanatory copy. Why: View person and Request currently sit beneath the much larger business-owned reference, far from Maya's own work on desktop. The objects remain correctly distinct, but the action's visual ownership is ambiguous.
- 6. **Hero pass-2 evidence package and final verification..** Supply the actual forward/reverse recordings after these corrections, including audience switching, Pause motion and keyboard operation. Include reduced-motion endpoints and identify the hardware/browser behind the reported frame timings. Why: The strips establish sampled movement and state changes, not smooth timing, focus behavior, decoder continuity or full performance.
