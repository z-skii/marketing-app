**scroll model.** Use one public stage coordinate system and stable media identities, with native document scrolling. **transitions.** Never animate blur, saturation, border geometry or shadow radius. A lens has a fixed filter and a separate opaque backing. Fade that backing from opacity 1 to 0 over 180ms only when real content is passing beneath it; return it to opacity 1 before long reading or commitment. The text backing remains opaque throughout. An opaque campaign sheet may expose a 32px translucent lip, but its body never becomes glass. Dark-to-light navigation uses a backing-opacity exchange at the stage boundary, not two overlapping filtered navigation bars. Under reduced transparency, unsupported filtering or failed contrast tests, use solid #FFFFFF or #171B1D.

**shadows and edges.** Depth comes from overlap, a visible 1px contact edge, one broad physical shadow and different travel distances. Use no ornamental glow and no gradient-filled panels. A 1px masked edge highlight may be brighter at the top-left and quieter at the lower-right, but it never travels like a scanner. All shadow layers are pre-rendered and move with their objects. Optical refraction is restricted to a registered duplicate of the same static photograph in the 6px rim; never duplicate text, a QR or a video decoder.

**principle.** Make translucency materially noticeable by putting real product content behind a very small number of lenses. The hero can be deep without being glass: photography, opaque earnings terms and a coded program object do most of the work. Navigation and an active inspection rail earn transparency; money, content, people, customer identity, forms and attribution do not. This takes the supplied Artec study's material restraint, not its clouds, purple mesh, metallic headings or ambient loops.

## thesis

Make TapMart feel like work, media, money and customer relationships changing hands in one physical space. A coffee reference comes forward from a composition of real product objects, becomes a separate creator's work, and ends at truthful earning terms. A beautifully photographed everyday car becomes a specific advertising placement. A customer's returning card finally gives the foreground back to the creator who brought them in. The distinction is not decoration: visitors keep scrolling because they can follow an object and anticipate its next consequence.
