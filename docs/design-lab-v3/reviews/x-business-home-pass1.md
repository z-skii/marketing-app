The feed is bright, uncovered and predominantly photographic. The phone navigation reads as a restrained frosted foreground over real car imagery, not blanket glassmorphism. That restraint is worth keeping. The interiors are less convincing: a large generic white inspection shell, rounded plan/rate boxes and stacked sheets replace object-specific hierarchy. More blur would not fix this.
