## The ten questions

- Does this feel like a premium modern product? **yes**. The photography, open canvas and restrained controls feel considered. Desktop seam spacing needs one final tightening.
- Does it explain itself without paragraphs? **yes**. People, work, asking rate and actions are immediately readable. The Loyalty entry explains enrollment and return in one short line.
- Does it feel like a consumer platform, not business software? **yes**. Faces and work lead. There are no dashboard tiles, administrative headings or metric grids.
- Is it memorable? **yes**. The portrait/work pairing and the quiet return seam create a recognizable composition rather than a generic feed.
- Does motion improve understanding? **partly**. Not assessable from these static captures. No motion strip demonstrates selection, opening, count replacement or reduced motion.
- Does each earning type feel different? **partly**. People and vehicle opportunities have clearly different silhouettes. Recreate versus Story earning surfaces are not shown.
- Is business discovery exciting? **yes**. Large faces, tangible work samples and a complete vehicle photograph make discovery inviting without promotional noise.
- Is Profile identity, not settings? **partly**. Profile is not captured. The Business navigation label cannot establish its destination's quality.
- Does the website make someone keep scrolling? **partly**. The public website is outside these captures. The marketplace continuation is inviting, but that is a different judgment.
- Is it significantly stronger than current production? **partly**. The visible Loyalty entry strengthens the business story without displacing discovery. Full comparative and operational superiority is not established by these screenshots.

## The five Loyalty questions

- Does this feel like a natural part of TapMart? **yes**. It uses the same canvas, artwork language, navigation and quiet actions. Its placement connects discovery and retention without changing Home's purpose.
- Does it strengthen the business value proposition? **yes**. Members and Came back introduce an understandable outcome beyond finding someone to advertise the shop.
- Can a business understand it quickly? **yes**. The live entry is immediate, and both alternative states give the appropriate next action. Opening phone copy through Maya's actions is approximately 26 words including navigation labels and badges.
- Does it feel consumer quality rather than SaaS admin? **yes**. A small branded object and ordinary language replace statistic cards and management controls.
- Does attribution feel powerful without becoming fake analytics? **partly**. The two counts are restrained and make no revenue or causality claim. Source attribution, definitions and reconciliation are not visible on this Home entry.

## Why better than production

This adds a clear bridge from advertising the shop to bringing customers back. A business can still find people and cars first, then see how many people joined its loyalty program and returned. Loyalty feels like part of running the same business, not another software product. These captures demonstrate that entry and its honest setup states, not live customer acquisition, sales attribution or Wallet delivery.
