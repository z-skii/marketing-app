## Material read

The feed is mostly uncovered photography on a bright neutral canvas, with opaque ink actions and a single translucent phone navigation bar. The bar visibly picks up the car underneath; glass is rationed rather than applied to content. Loyalty and money correctly stay unglazed. Desktop tasks are readable opaque paper over a dim scrim, but their oversized white shells lack the compositional confidence of the feed. This is not cheap glassmorphism; it is restrained material with unfinished inspection geometry.
