# TapMart V2: the directions

Explored and chosen by Astra, TapMart's design director. Source: directions.json.

## Founder read

TapMart already has the necessary product: one account, Personal and Business contexts, three real earning workflows, and a separate subscription content service. The redesign is not permission to invent a social network, simplify away financial obligations or reorganise the primary destinations. The change is from explanation-first screens to recognisable work, visible compensation and one next action, with administration behind one gear. Profile must become a person with proof of work; Business Home must become an attractive marketplace; the website must demonstrate rather than document. I have studied the supplied brief, measured study, product truth, inventory, four production captures and three media assets. The production captures corroborate the heaviness but have no styling authority. I have not inspected the repository, live backend or Artec pixels independently. This is a direction specification, not a claim that screens, files, measurements or motion recordings already exist. Build only in the isolated lab, guarded to return 404 in production unless DESIGN_LAB=1. Proposed experience routes are /design-lab-v2, /design-lab-v2/home, /design-lab-v2/me and /design-lab-v2/business. Do not alter production UI, backend behaviour, legacy code or production account state. Complete browser captures, motion checks, revisions and the V2 report before any approval to proceed.

## Artec lessons

- Access honesty: the supplied study had search snippets and third-party product descriptions, not access to Artec's pixels or motion. I have not independently inspected Artec in a browser. These lessons concern its documented product model and copy, not claimed visual observations.
- Make one clear promise per audience. TapMart's public opening should communicate earning; its business chapter should communicate access to real people, cars and content.
- Show work before credentials. A business should first see what a person makes, not a compressed database row of followers, verification and review metadata.
- A request is the bridge from discovery to a relationship. Keep the person, opportunity, conversation and subsequent state connected rather than scattering contact actions.
- Reveal sensitive or detailed information because a person has taken a relevant action. This is not permission to hide eligibility, price, deadlines or consequences before commitment.
- Let the number lead only when it is real and meaningful. TapMart should foreground actual compensation and balances, not borrow an invented creator score.
- Use short declarative language with verbs. Do not explain the mechanism before showing the outcome.
- Borrow the discipline, not an imagined visual style. TapMart must express three physically different earning activities and a separate Monthly Content service that Artec's documented model does not define.

## Direction: Open Cut

**Thesis.** TapMart is a place where ordinary things become paid opportunities: making a piece of content, posting a finished creative or offering space on a real car. Open Cut makes that conversion visible. The image carries the first impression, the amount establishes the value, and one clean edge opens the actual work. ## Chosen

**Open Cut.** Open Cut best balances the founder's ten questions. Night Channel has the strongest cinematic first impression but weakens comparison, empty states and the distinction between earning types. Market Index is efficient but too close to the directory and documentation feeling being rejected. Open Cut can be immediately visual on phone, deliberately composed on desktop, inviting for both sides of the marketplace and truthful about serious money. It makes Profile into identity without inventing a portrait, gives each earning type a different physical silhouette and gives motion a semantic job. Its advantage is structural rather than a palette refresh. Selection is approval of the direction to prototype, not final approval of unbuilt screens.

Merged:

- From Night Channel: a focused dark media viewer entered deliberately from a reference, Story or vehicle image. This improves inspection without turning the entire app into a streaming interface.
- From Market Index: disciplined factual action bands and scroll-anchor preservation when real records arrive. This improves clarity and stability without importing directory rows or a permanent enterprise-style inspector.
- No merged palette, second display family, cinematic app navigation, invented creator score, equal-card grid or decorative car treatment. The selected system remains one coherent identity.

Stays from Frame Shift:

- The media-and-money relationship in the leading User Home opportunity. It already connects the work to its value; V2 makes the media larger and removes duplicated explanation.
- The concise three-action public proposition: Recreate, Post, Drive. These remain useful public verbs, while internal kinds and workflow truth remain unchanged.
- The business marketplace foundation of real people, work samples and cars. This is better than replacing Home with a management dashboard.
- The distinct business actions View person and Request. One inspects the person; the other starts a real invitation. Their surrounding metadata is reduced, not their purpose.
- Real financial figures, real connected-state distinctions, real vehicle photography and honest absence of 3D. These are product strengths, not disclaimers to erase.
- The four Personal destinations and five Business destinations, including the separate Monthly Content destination and lime Create action. Navigation familiarity is useful; Frame Shift's particular visual treatment is not preserved.
- The idea of showing genuine product UI on the public site. V2 enlarges and choreographs readable coded UI rather than relying on a tiny, text-heavy phone screenshot.
