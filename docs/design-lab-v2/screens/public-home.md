# V2 art direction: Public Homepage

By Astra, TapMart's design director.

**Two seconds.** Your everyday can earn: make a Reel, post a Story, or put advertising on a car. Photography establishes those three activities immediately; businesses have a clear second entrance.

**Signature.** An everyday image opens along the earning edge into its own paid-work preview: a reference frame, an intact Story and a photographed car retain different silhouettes, but meet the same straight money-and-action boundary. The public page makes that transformation legible at human scale, rather than putting three tiny marketplace screenshots inside floating phones. The money is an already labelled example, never a reward produced by scrolling.
