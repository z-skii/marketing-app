# V2 art direction: User Profile

By Astra, TapMart's design director.

**Two seconds.** This is Maya: her work, what she has earned here, and her listed car. It is unmistakably a fictional profile, not an account-settings page.

**Signature.** The finished-work cut: a portrait submission, an intact Story and a landscape placement photograph end on one straight, brick-tipped edge. On desktop, the person's lifetime Earned figure begins directly below that baseline in the identity column. It connects a person to completed work without turning the profile into a marketplace listing or suggesting that the visible samples constitute a payout.

- Recreate reads as authored content: the largest portrait-format work sample, with no play icon because only a submission still exists. Opening it reveals Recreate, its fictional business, Approved, the approval date and credited earnings.
